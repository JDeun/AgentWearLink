# Meta glasses firmware/DSP wake feasibility — public evidence decision

Reviewed 2026-11-25 for [#661](https://github.com/JDeun/AgentWearLink/issues/661). Scope: public primary sources and previously pinned public SDK/companion source only. No firmware extraction, modified package installation, signature bypass, exploit reproduction, credentials, hardware experiment or private protocol implementation. No device model/build is inferred for an untested capability.

## Correction to the previous abstract-only record

The [November 6, 2026 researcher write-up](https://p0pcycle.com/2026/11/06/hacking-the-meta-ray-ban-smartglasses/) now supplies a first-hand technical report complementing the [Nullcon abstract](https://nullcon.net/talk/beeps-and-patches-arbitrary-code-execution-on-meta-ray-ban/). The [conference program](https://nullcon.net/event/nullcon-berlin-2026/schedule/) identifies the presentation. The write-up describes an Android-based firmware and code-signing security research on Ray-Ban Meta glasses, reports limited application-context execution, and reports loss of the initial access as firmware behavior changed. The researcher states the issue was reported through Meta's bounty program and infers a fix from publication approval. This is a researcher report, not independent AWL reproduction or a vendor-confirmed current affected/fixed-build matrix. No root-level or privileged DSP model replacement, steady-state custom wake inference, battery comparison, Korean wake recognition, safe rollback, supported distributable installation or privacy-indicator equivalence was demonstrated by that report. No operational exploit details are retained in AWL.

The earlier [third-party audit](third-party-wake-source-audit-2026-11-25.md) recorded an abstract-only gap; this report supersedes that evidence classification, not its conclusion about DSP access.

## Decision and reopening conditions

- **GO:** read-only public evidence review and authorized, non-invasive observations using the documented SDK and user-consented owned hardware.
- **NO-GO:** firmware modification, signature bypass, unsupported application installation, privilege escalation or DSP model replacement as an AWL research prototype or production architecture.
- **DEFER:** on-device custom wake via a documented manufacturer-supported enrollment contract, or a separately authorized, independently reviewed companion route. #648, #660, #663 and #665 own those gates; #655/#658 retain the empirical product decision and controlled power/accuracy comparisons. #637 retains action authorization, privacy and release security.
- Baseline remains official invocation/manual fallback and explicitly bounded opt-in host listening where supported; no always-on high-power route as default.

The research objective is to decide feasibility and boundaries, not demonstrate an unsupported modification. The primary technical publication gap is resolved, but the exact proprietary DSP implementation, current patch/model matrix, warranty applicability, physical wake/power and distribution approval remain unknown. Close #661 as completed static **no-go** decision after merging this record; reopen only on an authorized public enrollment/firmware interface, material new lawful non-operational technical evidence that changes the privilege/capability assessment, or a concrete supported integration plan. Do not close empirical or companion gates on this report.
