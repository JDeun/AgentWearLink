## Correct scope: VIDEO, not a mobile pairing SDK

Pinned [Android README](https://github.com/facebookincubator/muse-gadget-sdk/blob/1b894f551e64416e2844452ec6056ec56d6a10f1/android/musegadgetstest/README.md) and [iOS README](https://github.com/facebookincubator/muse-gadget-sdk/blob/1b894f551e64416e2844452ec6056ec56d6a10f1/ios/musegadgetstest/README.md) describe the same phone-camera/color-bars/sample clip sender for a Muse display over **local Wi-Fi TCP**. The 40 candidate paths are under musegadgetstest. They do not implement the encrypted BLE provisioning, backend VM credential lifecycle or Noise control session. They therefore do not close #641 direct cross-language pairing/Noise acceptance or imply iOS Meta glasses access. The relevant receiving implementation is muse_video_wifi.c / muse_video_wire.c / muse_video.c / muse_vstream, not noise_control.cpp. [VIDEO protocol](https://github.com/facebookincubator/muse-gadget-sdk/blob/1b894f551e64416e2844452ec6056ec56d6a10f1/protocols/VIDEO.md#L101-L189) explicitly states no authentication or encryption, and mDNS replies are not identities. Board-view activation constrains a display action, but is not sender consent, access grant, confidentiality or trusted device identity.
